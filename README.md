# Broken Heart

**Alpha 1.47**

A 2D survival and crafting game built with Unity. Explore forests and caves, gather materials, and build a workshop to craft tools and smelt ore.

## Play in Unity

Open the repository folder as a project in Unity Hub using Unity 6000.6.9f1. Open `Assets/Game/Scenes/Menu.unity` and press Play.

Use A and D to move, Space to jump, E or Tab to open the inventory, and M to open the map. Use the mouse wheel to cycle through the hotbar.

The game saves every two minutes of play, after a full rest, and when you return to the menu. F5 saves manually and F9 reloads the last save. Monster encounters and terrain shadows are paused in this alpha.
